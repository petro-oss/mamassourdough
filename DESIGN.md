# Mama's Sourdough — Design (UX/UI)

## Direction

Brand built from scratch for this project — warm, artisan, homely, community-focused. No stock "corporate bakery" feel; leans into real photography and hand-crafted warmth.

## Reference sites

TBC — no specific reference sites recorded; direction was developed iteratively through the build.

## Style

**Colours:**
```
cream:    #FAF6F0  (page background)
cream-dk: #F2EAE0  (section backgrounds)
brown:    #2C1A0E  (primary text, dark panels)
brown-md: #4A2E1A  (body text)
tan:      #8B6347  (secondary text)
amber:    #C4852A  (CTA buttons, icon bubbles, accents)
green:    #4A6741  (community/values accent)
warm:     #D4A96A  (subtle warm accent)
```

**Fonts:** DM Sans (headings/body), Cormorant Garamond italic (quotes), DM Mono (labels)

**Tone/feel:** Warm, artisan, community-led — amber circle icon bubbles, SVG line-art icons (no emoji), scroll-reveal fade-up animations, count-up stats. No em dashes in visible copy.

## Page notes

### Home
Health benefits section (interactive 8-card grid, click to expand), Values section (Community / Love & Giving / Patience, amber number bubbles), Features section (4 cards, amber icon bubbles), animated stats row (10+, 4, 100%, ∞), scroll-reveal on sections.

### About
Rosie's (Lucie's daughter) painting of sourdough bread on the "love letter" panel. No personal photos of Lucie.

### Menu
Loaf varieties split out individually (Country White, Wholemeal, Seeded, Rye, Soft White Tin), plus cookies/banana loaf/focaccia/muffins. Allergen info shown per product plus a green allergen notice banner. Updated weekly.

### Order
Order form with recurring/weekly standing order checkbox; allergen notice banner; collection/delivery details confirmed on placement.

### Contact
Standard contact details/form.
